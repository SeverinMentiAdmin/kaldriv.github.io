About

The person doing the work is the person you talk to.

I'm Severin Menti, a Swiss management consultant specialising in Temenos core banking implementations. Over ten years I've led programmes across Switzerland, Luxembourg, and Asia-Pacific — from initial scoping through to go-live.

Kaldriv OÜ is my independent consultancy, registered in Estonia. It exists to give banks access to senior Temenos expertise without the overhead of a large SI. You get direct engagement, clear communication, and someone who has navigated these implementations before.

I'm based in Tallinn and work primarily in the DACH and Baltic markets, with flexibility for remote engagements across Europe.
